Plan your curriculum in four steps

  1. Choose your settings

    First select your specialisation, path and previous education. Required courses are then preselected for you.

  2. Add courses and projects

    Add courses from the left bar using the "+" button next to the course and choose a year to add it, or drag a block onto the calendar canvas. On your phone, use Add courses & projects. Courses not in the standard list can be added by the "Add unlisted course" button and inserting all required information.

  3. Adjust your planning

    Select a block to change its year. For research projects and literature thesis you can also change starting month to some extent. Research projects can also include leave and part-time work.

  4. Check, save and export

    Review the 120 EC total and warnings. Autosave keeps changes in this browser; "Save my curriculum planning" exports your planning to a file that can be reimported later using "Open a saved planning". Undo reverses changes. If you reach a programme of 120 EC you can also export a PDF with a visual overview of your planned curriculum. "Export for PEP approval" creates a file you can open in the separate PEP tool for the approval process by the specialisation coordinator and examination board.

Change plan settings?

Required courses will be reloaded and your current selections replaced. You can recover your current plan with Undo.
